# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-21

Fixed
- Episode Queue: opening a show's episodes closes the one that was open, so only one show is open at a time (#1)
- Marking episodes on a show's page now updates the Episode Queue for shows TMDB numbers across seasons, like One Piece (season 23 starts at episode 1156). Episodes marked the old way are moved over (#2)
- Upcoming Episodes, the Calendar and episode lists show the day an episode comes out where you are: anime airing after midnight in Japan no longer show up a day late (#3)
- Report a bug or request a feature on phones: the form now opens inside the app, so the type (bug or feature) and the description reach GitHub, not just the title (#5)
- Caught Up: a finished show that gets a new season announced or new episodes goes back to your Watchlist, and shows in Caught Up while you wait (#7)

New
- Where to watch: tapping a service opens the show or movie on that service (The Boys on Prime Video), not just the list of services (#4)
- Library: titles that aren't out yet show when they come out ("Upcoming • October, 2027" and a countdown), or "Unknown Release Date" (#6)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

First release.

- A new look inspired by Showline: condensed headlines; show pages with Details, Progress and Cast & Crew tabs
- Shows: your pinned Watchlist (scrolls sideways with the mouse wheel), an Episode Queue you can open and scroll through, this week's episodes and upcoming seasons
- Movies: your Watchlist with runtimes, upcoming releases and recently watched
- Library at a glance: shows in Watchlist, Watch Later, Caught Up and Finished; movies in Watchlist, Watch Later and Watched
- Wallpapers: six built in, or import your own picture (Settings → Theme & Wallpaper)
- Discover: recommendations from your library, trending and most anticipated
- Search your library or all of TMDB, with recent searches
- Show and movie pages: progress, seasons, cast, where to watch, collections, more like this
- Trailers and clips play in the app, with Open in YouTube
- Episode Scorecard
- Calendar of new episodes and movie releases: list, week and month views, with new-episode alerts on desktop
- History with watch time by month
- Discover and Search
- Sync with Google
- Report a bug or request a feature from the app (Send feedback in the sidebar, or Settings)
- A new app icon
- In-app updates
