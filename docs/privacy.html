Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Your data stays yours

WatchList stores your library (the shows and movies you add), your watch history and your settings on your own device. There are no WatchList servers and no accounts, and nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Sync with Google (optional)

If you turn on Sync with Google, WatchList asks for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and your email address (to show which account is syncing). WatchList keeps one file there with your WatchList data so your devices stay the same. It cannot see or touch any other files in your Drive. Turn Sync off at any time to sign out; you can delete the file by removing WatchList under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

WatchList’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connections

To look up shows, movies, episodes, people, artwork and streaming availability, WatchList calls The Movie Database (TMDB) API (api.themoviedb.org and image.tmdb.org). These requests contain the title or ID being looked up, your chosen language and region, and no personal data. Update checks go to GitHub. TMDB’s own privacy policy applies to its service.
